MONDAY v0.1

Design Direction: Quiet Computing

Design a complete, production-quality web application called Monday.

This is not an AI chat application.

This is not a productivity dashboard.

This is not a virtual assistant.

Monday is a persistent digital companion designed to accompany people through the difficult beginnings of creative work.

The product exists to make difficult beginnings feel less overwhelming.

⸻

THE STORY

Everyone has a Monday.

Sometimes it’s Monday morning.

Sometimes it’s opening an empty IDE.

Sometimes it’s staring at a blank document.

Sometimes it’s trying to start a difficult project.

Monday exists for those moments.

It doesn’t replace your thinking.

It thinks beside you.

The interface should make the user feel welcomed back every time they open it.

It should feel like sitting down with a warm cup of coffee before continuing meaningful work.

DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

Design for quiet confidence.

Every screen should communicate:

“I remember where we left off.”

Avoid making the interface feel transactional.

Avoid flashy visuals.

Avoid “AI” aesthetics.
